# The-Limoges-City-Hall-Fire
On the afternoon of Saturday, October 17, 2026, a national protest day in France turned violent in a dozen cities. In Limoges — a quiet provincial city of about 130,000 people in west-central France — a group of masked people broke into the city hall, set it on fire, and watched as the 143-year-old building's bell tower collapsed into the flames.
